================================================================================
                            EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

FarmSquare is a B2B agricultural marketplace platform connecting farmers 
directly with buyers in Nigeria. The platform eliminates middlemen, ensures 
fair pricing, and provides quality verification through a network of field 
agents.

KEY OBJECTIVES:
- Connect farmers directly with buyers
- Ensure quality verification at source
- Provide secure escrow payment system
- Enable transparent order tracking
- Support multiple user roles (Farmer, Buyer, Agent, Admin)

================================================================================
                            BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

KYC/KYB VERIFICATION:
- Farmers must complete KYC before withdrawals
- Buyers must complete KYB before placing orders
- Admin can approve/reject with reason
- Rejected users can resubmit

LISTINGS:
- Only active listings appear in marketplace
- Quantity cannot be reduced below active orders
- Paused listings hidden from buyers

ORDERS:
- Orders create escrow automatically
- Rejected orders trigger automatic refund
- Delivered orders release funds to farmer
- Buyer must confirm delivery

PAYMENTS:
- All payments held in escrow
- Funds released on delivery confirmation
- Refunds processed automatically on rejection
- Withdrawals require KYC approval
